### Generation and identifiers

- There is no separate subcategory table or subcategory field in this seed configuration. The product-type lists below are the six configured name templates per category, not persisted subcategories.
- Each category produces 6 product types x 5 variants = 30 products. Names follow `{brand} {product_type} {variant}`.
- SKU format is `PN-SYN-{category_index:02d}-{type_index:02d}{variant_index:02d}` with all three indices one-based in the emitted SKU.
- A category's six-brand pool is assigned cyclically using `(type_index * number_of_variants + variant_index) % number_of_brands`; each configured category has 30 products and each of its six brands is used five times.
- Category, store, product, image, and inventory IDs are UUIDv5 values derived from the fixed namespace `5d77f1cb-0e91-4bbf-91bb-11fb96b08c1e` and stable key strings.
- Product `created_at` is `2025-01-01T00:00:00Z + (product_index % 365) days`, with a zero-based global product index. `is_active` is false when `product_index % 17 == 0`, true otherwise (32 inactive and 508 active across this dataset).

## Images

- A new image row is primary with `display_order=0`. On an existing catalogue, an image is inserted only when a matched product has no image rows; existing images are preserved. Therefore 540 is the fresh-target count, not a promise to insert 540 rows on every run.

## Limitations and demo-only values

- Product names are generated combinations of brand labels, generic product types, and variants; they are synthetic records, not verified commercial SKUs. Brand labels include names that may correspond to real-world brands. The seeder does not establish affiliation, authorization, or whether any exact product exists.
- Every description explicitly identifies a synthetic PATRONET comparison record and states that its brand label/specifications are not claims about a real commercial SKU.
- Every specification object includes `data_provenance: "synthetic_demo"`. Processor names are explicitly synthetic. Nutrition, ingredients, materials, dimensions, device parameters, and product use details are illustrative, not verified package/device claims.
- Appliance star ratings are marked synthetic and the generated description says they are not certified. Food/pet nutrition and allergen data are illustrative and must not be used for consumption or safety decisions.
- Store names, addresses, PIN codes, and UUIDs are seed values. Their real-world address/retail status was not verified.
- Placeholder image links are not actual product images. Stock quantities, product active flags, prices, and timestamps are generated demo values, not operational data.
- Price columns contain numeric amounts only; INR is inferred from PATRONET's project context. Neither the Product model nor this seeder stores a currency code.

- No database connection was opened and no database records were read or written for this report. No credentials or secrets are included.
- No claim is made here that placeholder URL reachability, store address validity, brand rights, or product-specification accuracy was externally verified.
